Salute, then en garde

You fence from the left in red. Close the distance, lunge when the target is in reach, and parry to earn a riposte.

Controls

D / →
Advance one step (hold to keep stepping)
A / ←
Retreat one step
Space / J
Lunge. Right after a successful parry it becomes a fast riposte.
K / S
Parry. Only the first ~0.25 s blocks; a wasted parry leaves you open.

How to score

  • Lunge distance is about 2.6 m between bodies. From the en garde lines you need three or four advances.
  • Attack into preparation. The box cannot parry mid-step, so hit it as it advances.
  • Draw the parry. Step in to bait a parry, then lunge while it recovers.
  • Watch the back line. Cross the end of the piste with both feet and the touch goes to your opponent.
